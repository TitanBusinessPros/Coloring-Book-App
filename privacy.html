Privacy Policy

Artistic Coloring Studio · Last updated September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Artistic Coloring Studio (the "App"), provided by Titan Business Pros LLC ("we," "us," "our"), handles when you use it, and how it's handled. We built the App to need as little of your information as possible.

1. Information From Google Sign-In

The App requires you to sign in with a Google account before you can use it, using Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with the App so we can display "signed in as ___" and let you sign out. That information is used only in your browser, for that purpose — we do not have a database of our own that stores it, and we do not see or keep a copy of it on any server we control.

Your Google account and sign-in session are governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

2. Your Uploaded Images and Downloaded Artwork

If you upload your own picture to color, it is read and processed entirely inside your web browser (using your browser's local file reader) — it is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate. Likewise, when you click "Download as PNG," the file is generated and saved directly by your browser to your device. We have no access to, and keep no copy of, anything you upload or create in the App. This project includes Firestore/Storage security rules for possible future use, but they are not currently connected to any part of the App.

3. Coloring Page Images

The App's built-in coloring page gallery loads images from galleries we maintain. If an image can't be loaded directly, the App may request it through a third-party image-fetching service (images.weserv.nl) as a fallback so the picture still displays correctly. That request includes the image's web address and is subject to that service's own privacy practices; we don't control what that service logs.

4. Hosting and Standard Web Logs

The App is hosted on GitHub Pages and, as a synced secondary copy, on Firebase Hosting. Like virtually any website, these hosting providers may automatically log standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) for security and operational purposes. We do not separately collect or review this data ourselves.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We do not set our own tracking or advertising cookies. Google/Firebase Authentication may store sign-in session information in your browser (such as via local storage) so you stay signed in — this is part of how Google Sign-In works and is not used by us for tracking or advertising.

6. Analytics

The App does not currently run any analytics or tracking on your usage. If that changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy first.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience coloring activity, but it does require a Google sign-in, which is itself subject to Google's own age requirements and policies for account holders. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children beyond what Google Sign-In provides as described above.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
